- (direction) Derive the canonical contract from the finalized PRD and the Architecture Spine; downstream must read both adopted companions.
- (capability) CAP-1: A documentary researcher can manually retrieve qualifying public events from the three approved Sources so the team no longer searches source websites manually.
- (capability) CAP-2: A documentary researcher can review, explain, prioritise, approve, or reject Candidate Events so editorial judgment remains accountable and auditable.
- (capability) CAP-3: Editors and journalists can view a clear 14-calendar-day agenda of approved events so they no longer maintain the Word agenda document manually.
- (capability) CAP-4: The team can see source failures, retain earlier research, and use clearly labelled fixtures so a source outage does not undermine the demo.
- (constraint) MVP is a one-time internal hackathon browser demo with no authentication, manual retrieval only, human approval before publication, and exactly three Sources: Federal President, Federal Constitutional Court, and UN Women.
- (constraint) Federal President retrieval is Terminkalender-page first then official appointment RSS fallback; UN Women is one Source with News and Publications Channels, each page first then its supplied official RSS fallback.
- (constraint) Events must deduplicate, retain review history, use transparent 1–5 suggestions, and include only today through the next 13 calendar days when approved events are shown in the agenda.
- (event) SPEC coherence and preservation pass: all finalized PRD capabilities, constraints, non-goals, and demo success signals have an explicit landing place in the kernel or adopted companions.
